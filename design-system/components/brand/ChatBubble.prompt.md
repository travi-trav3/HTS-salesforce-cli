One-line: The speech-bubble conversation motif — the visual language of the product's messaging.

```jsx
<ChatBubble role="orange" tail="right">What time does the range close?</ChatBubble>
<ChatBubble role="incoming">Tee time confirmed for 9:40 — see you on the green.</ChatBubble>
```

- Roles: `incoming` (slate), `outgoing` (azure), `orange`, `green`, `outline`. `tail`: `left | right | none`.
- Slate is the calm conversation surface — don't swap it for the azure CTA blue.
